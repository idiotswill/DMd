## Revision evidence

The official [2025-05-01 community update](https://www.dndbeyond.com/community-update) identifies 5.2.1 as the correction to the 2025-04-22 SRD 5.2 release: it restores fifteen omitted magic items, replaces a duplicate Iron Golem stat block with Knight, adds Octopus, and corrects page numbering/legal layout. The [official conversion guide](https://media.dndbeyond.com/compendium-images/srd/guide/converting-to-srd-5.2.1.pdf), published 2025-05-27, is supplementary migration guidance, not another content source for this pack.

The SRD FAQ says the 5.2 release includes second-printing errata available at release and later SRD versions may incorporate later errata. This pin does **not** claim that every later core-book erratum is already in 5.2.1. Apply a changed rule only after reviewing a legally reusable source/version and compatibility; do not silently scrape updated Basic Rules.

## Content boundaries and decisions

- The full selected SRD's rules/content families are accounted for, including optional/toolbox material. A ledger deferral is an implementation schedule, not a license exclusion or a product-scope reduction.
- Do not copy non-SRD character options, settings, art or monsters. The official FAQ identifies omissions such as Artificer, Aasimar and Beholder; DMd 1.0 does not promise those proprietary options. Original compatible substitutes, if desired later, need their own provenance and cannot copy omitted expression.
- Preserve the licensed SRD names, including renamed items/spells; do not restore proprietary book names for familiarity.
- Use selected official default rules. Campaign optional rules/house rules require explicit persisted selection; their implementation/evidence is separately owned. No language-model explanation can alter this pin or adjudication authority.
- No unresolved legal-source blocker was found for this pinned CC BY path. Future non-SRD additions require separate evidence. Gate 13 owns release notices/pack distribution verification, and Gate 14 owns end-to-end rules coverage acceptance.
