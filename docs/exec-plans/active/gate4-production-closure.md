## Reusable spell work beyond the reviewed baseline

Immediate source casting, real Hold Person groups, rays, concentration children and
reviewed breath saves/shared amounts advance the existing mechanism matrix. They
do not establish the following missing production paths:

- Shield/Counterspell/nested interruption and all actual Ready release/ignore/move/
  attack/held-magic paths; legitimate source grants, correct timing and reservation
  rules are required. Descriptor availability alone is insufficient.
- Fixed/attached zone geometry, moved-zone versus creature entry, exclusions and
  per-target-per-turn uses; movement/attack/casting/interaction triggers. Occupant
  IDs alone are not authoritative zone geometry.
- Walls/panels/side damage/opacity/collision/section HP/burning and expiry; distinct
  intangible spell proxies with their own position/source and repeated activations.
- Forced/compelled movement, teleport, removal/reappearance and carried belongings;
  observer-specific illusions/discovery; safe source-linked behavioral constraints
  with material PC decisions retained by their controllers.
- Source summons, controller/command costs/initiative/lifetime; forms that retain
  the actual identity while replacing permitted statistics/anatomy/gear and tracking
  source-owned temporary HP/reversion/concentration.
- Haste/Slow/restricted extra actions and reaction limits; damage transfer, actual-
  damage-linked healing, dispel and linked suppression; mixed/recursive effect
  programs, extra dice and complete source Multiattack/legendary execution.

Next useful shared primitive after the reaction checkpoint is source zone anchors
and movement/turn contact, coordinated with forced displacement and weapon riders.
Do not add a second resolver or fake creature identity for an intangible proxy.
