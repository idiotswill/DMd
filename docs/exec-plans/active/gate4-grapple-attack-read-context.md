## Existing records, exact producers and complete membership

Use existing `GrappleReadCut`, `GrappleCutKey`, `GrappleReader`, immutable
`TacticalGrip` proofs and `GrappleEndReceipt`. No new serialized field is planned.
Old absent attachment JSON, raw roles/UUID domains and original source pins stay
exact. Empty optional attachments remain omitted; no generic empty-cut opt-in.

The underlying algorithm has privately constructed current and authenticated
read contexts. Neither is deserializable, supplied by a client, or constructed
from a caller's chosen grip IDs/reservation mask. Live admission always reads
current truth. Retained reconstruction overlays only the new-grip contribution;
it does not replace unrelated conditions, Dodge records, physical equipment,
source capabilities, visibility, geometry, AC, costs or ammunition with old data.

Complete AttackAdmission membership is the sorted unique union of:

1. All established live incoming relations on the attacker, including a relation
   whose holder is this target and relations whose disadvantage currently cancels
   another advantage. Membership does not depend on the final RollMode.
2. All established live incoming relations on the target, because the common
   attack reader checks target Grappled/Dodge. The real permanent Dodge ending
   remains authoritative; this overlay must never restore it after release.
3. All established live outgoing hand reservations of the attacker consumed by
   physical weapon admission. An intrinsic source with a held implement uses
   this hand input; unarmed body damage with no implement does not invent one.

Use the actual source reader to choose the relevant categories; define that
closed mapping once and reuse it for capture and validation. Include every
relation in a relevant category, not only the chosen weapon hand or one holder.
Pure unarmed/intrinsic bodies do not acquire anatomy/hand requirements from the
helper. No provisional/future Attempt, unrelated body or ended proof enters a
fresh current cut. The global rule that ordinary Grapple requires no resolution
prevents creating a new grip inside already admitted attack work; retain that
rule and test it.

At `attacks::begin_with_source`, `unarmed::begin`, and
`creature::begin_creature_attack`, derive source facts, membership and original
hands before payment in the real preparation. Printed `begin_creature_weapon`
flows through that same physical admission. After all checks succeed, commit the
existing cost/equipment/ammo, install the actual attack/resolution and allocate
its AttackRoll through `turns::push_frame`. Before pump, associate the captured
proofs/cut with that exact newly registered work. Validate its actual node and
origin; never guess `next_occurrence`, allocate a parallel node, or search for
the most recent node of a kind. Failed preparation leaves no cut or payment.

For these four own-turn families, `AttackAdmission { attack }` uses the actual
attack origin, equal to the resolution origin. Its work is this AttackRoll node,
`issued_by` is the actual admitted command, and `source_attack` is None. Copy
complete immutable proofs; while live, retained and live copies must be equal.
Use no empty cut when the reader consumed no new relation authority. Unrelated
live grips still receive common validation without fabricating an attack cut.

At the real `continuations::start_inner` request-issue cut, after entering the
actual AttackRoll/AttackDamage work, derive the request using its authenticated
admission and record `RequestIssue { roll }` only when it consumes that authority.
Its source is the direct same-attack AttackAdmission with identical membership;
validate exact key, role, subject, work and same-node/descendant ancestry. The
source cannot inherit and another RequestIssue cannot be its parent authority.
Validation/request inspection is read-only and never produces a cut.

Capture the actual issuing command separately from the raw's mechanical cause.
After Shield, `PendingRoll.issued_by` for damage deliberately remains the accepted
hit's earlier `damage_cause`; the new cut's `issued_by` is the actual transition
that issues damage now. If release intervened, the new request legitimately
inherits the earlier admission set. Check chronological command identities and
actual work lineage; do not require those grips still live at request issue or
pretend that the earlier raw cause is this later command.

Automatic miss completes without an Attack raw; fixed unarmed/intrinsic damage
may complete without a Damage raw. Do not fabricate RequestIssue or raw evidence
for those paths. Retain their real admission until its completion/consumer ends.
Inspiration edits accepted faces on the existing request; it does not create a
new attack admission or resample grip membership. Retain Savage Attacker and all
existing raw ownership/arithmetic checks without changing their old semantics.
