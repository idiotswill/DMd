DMd adaptations: the source's rules/content headings and references have been organized into a machine-readable inventory, gate ownership and implementation evidence. Selected rules and content have been adapted into typed executable mechanics and kernel.json definitions. These adaptations include derived values, dice resolution, combat and recovery primitives; they do not reproduce the complete source or all effects of each listed item/spell. Supported primitive scopes and remaining work are recorded in the Rules Coverage Ledger and ADR 018. The original SRD is unchanged. Later adaptations must retain this notice and record their changes.

The character-creation.json adaptation adds a limited level-1 Human Fighter Soldier creation catalog, source-priced equipment and source references. Creation and selected feature mechanics adapt the source's standard-array, proficiencies, Fighting Styles, Second Wind, Heroic Inspiration and Savage Attacker rules. Recorded grants do not imply full tactical mastery, weapon-property, tool or item execution; their remaining scope is recorded in ADR 023.

The tactical.json adaptation organizes source weapon, spell and selected creature stat blocks into typed data. Creature additions include Chimera mixed attacks and breath substitution, selected Adult Red Dragon spellcasting and Legendary Actions, Command, Scorching Ray, and Fireball. Exact source references and explicitly omitted stat-block clauses are retained in the definitions. The selected Mage adaptation adds Protective Magic with its shared three-use daily pool, Counterspell, Mage Armor and physical Wand/component identities. Printed prepared AC is distinguished from the unbuffed imported defense; actual preparation requires a campaign spell effect. The selected Night Hag adaptation preserves its printed statistics and at-will level-4 Magic Missile with only the source Material-component waiver; omitted traits and other actions, including Magic Resistance and Soul Bag, remain explicit. Source definitions and reusable scheduling primitives do not imply complete spell execution or a complete creature catalog.

The separate immutable air-elemental-v1.json adaptation represents the complete Air Elemental stat block (SRD pages 258-259), including Air Form, Thunderous Slam, Multiattack and Whirlwind. Exact statistics and immunities are available through pinned creation. Open-space movement and the intrinsic Slam use existing execution; Air Form shared-space/narrow-passage movement, Multiattack and Whirlwind have explicit unsupported-operation boundaries. Complete source representation does not claim complete creature or Shove execution. The historical tactical.json source catalog is unchanged.

The separate immutable ogre-v1.json adaptation represents the Ogre stat block (SRD page 312), including one Greatclub and three distinct Javelins. Its two printed actions use three closed forms: Greatclub, Javelin melee and Javelin thrown. The reviewed interpretation retains the named weapons' relevant Two-Handed/Thrown properties and annotates this source with TwoHandsV1; this is an explicit physical-capability interpretation, not a literal anatomical quotation or a rule for all Giants. The printed 2d8/2d6 damage belongs only to the exact Ogre source, not ordinary recovered equipment. The authored application runtime admits exact full-pin Ogre creation and connects the three physical attack forms, held-melee opportunity attacks, explicit encounter-enabled ground recovery and desktop equipment controls. ID-only legacy lookup remains closed, and the source attack paths require the supported encounter version. Combined production source and its new application acceptance scenarios remain UNCOMPILED/UNRUN; independent review, exact-head checks, genuine recovery and native play evidence remain required before feature acceptance. All earlier immutable creature files and source identities remain unchanged.

The separate immutable mage-v2.json adaptation preserves the original selected Mage source (SRD page305), including its exact statistics, Wand gear, Mage Armor and shared Protective Magic source data, and adds only TwoHandsV1. This is an explicitly reviewed ordinary-body engineering interpretation for this revision; the printed page does not enumerate hands, and Wand use, Humanoid/Wizard tags and empty loadout slots are not general anatomical authority. The original Mage definition/pin, legacy lookup/picker and recorded spell histories remain unchanged. Current source creation selects the new full pin; both immutable revisions and their distinct spell tuples remain resolvable. Existing omitted features remain omitted. Source admission is not complete Mage, outgoing Grapple or live Counterspell acceptance; new application controls are authored and unrun pending exact-head verification.
